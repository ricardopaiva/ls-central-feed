<strong>91164 Allow only one receipt copy when the receipt is declined (LSTS-47755)</strong>
<ul><li>Print only one copy of a receipt in LS Central Norway, even when you run on a W1 Business Central. If you try to print a second copy, the POS shows <b>Copy receipt has already been printed once</b>.</li><li>This applies whether the customer took the original receipt or declined it.</li></ul>
