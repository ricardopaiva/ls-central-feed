<strong>84138 Paper prescription for Active substance – The mapping of the prescribed active substance in the GUI is taken from the dispensed item and not from the active substance table</strong>
<ul><li>The prescribed active‑substance description in the GUI is now taken from the active substance selected in the Active Substance table, and not from the product chosen as the dispensed item.</li><li>The display of the prescribed active substance in the GUI is now consistent with how it is shown for e‑prescriptions.</li></ul>
<strong>83712 SIT - Space missing in additional information field on dosage label when Norwegian</strong>
<ul><li>Fixing a translation issue, adding a space missing in the additional information field on the dosage label.</li></ul>
<strong>83736 PDD details for magistral prescriptions are missing the mapping to instructions</strong>
<ul><li>Instructions are mapped to Posted Prescription Order Line and PDD Details.</li></ul>
<strong>82723 The field ‘Locked’ is missing on the page for PDD details for reference‑number prescriptions</strong>
<ul><li>Fixed an issue where the Locked Prescription field was not displayed in the PDD Details Page. The field is now shown correctly, matching the behavior from previous versions.</li></ul>
