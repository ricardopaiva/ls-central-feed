<strong>90641 POS Journal Line Charges Not Reflected During Hotel Settlement</strong>
<ul><li>Show the individual charges that a folio payment settles. When you pay a checked-out reservation's folio balance in full with Accrual or Checkout accounting, the charges now appear under the POS deposit payment line, like they did before version 28. The Hotel Detailed Invoice prints them too. A new Hotel Setup option, <b>Detail charges on deposit payment</b>, turns this on. It is off by default.</li><li><b>Action required by partners:</b>
<ul>
<li>To use it, turn on <b>Detail charges on deposit payment</b> in Hotel Setup. If you use a customized copy of the Hotel Detailed Invoice report, it needs the same change to print the detailed rows.</li>
</ul>
</li></ul>
