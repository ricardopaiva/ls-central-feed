<strong>92383 "Connection was disconnected before invocation result was recieved"</strong>
<ul><li>Stop false <b>Connection was disconnected before invocation result was received</b> banners after a tender declaration in LS Central Norway. The POS no longer shows device error banners when it reloads the hardware profile. Real connection losses and the <b>Drawer is open!</b> message still appear.</li></ul>
<strong>92626 B2B/B2C – Errors on receipts for invoice customers (in Norwegian)</strong>
<ul><li>Print the full delivery receipt header on Norwegian credit-sale receipts. <b>Utleveringskvittering</b> is no longer cut off, and the receipt now shows <b>IKKE KVITTERING FOR KJØP</b> without the extra <b>Salgskvittering</b> line.</li><li>A blank line now separates the header from the rest of the receipt. The Bokmål text now reads <b>IKKE</b>, and Nynorsk users keep <b>IKKJE</b> through a new Nynorsk translation.</li></ul>
