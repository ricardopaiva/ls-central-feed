<strong>88109 Cleanup prescription order page when in AE order</strong>
<ul><li>Updated Prescription Order pages to better support <b>Assistive Equipment (AE) orders</b>.<ul><li>Page titles are now dynamically adjusted for AE orders: <b>Assistive Equipment, Approved Assistive Equipment</b>, and <b>Posted Assistive Equipment</b>.</li><li>Prescription-specific fields and sections that are not relevant to AE orders are now hidden on:<ul><li>Prescription Order / Approved Prescription Order / Posted Prescription Order pages.</li><li>Edit Prescription Order Line page.</li></ul></li><li>Standard prescription orders remain unchanged.</li></ul></li><li>This update affects the <b>UI only</b>. No data, validation, or posting logic was changed.</li></ul>
