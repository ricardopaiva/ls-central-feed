<strong>85990 Group Reservation: manual Unit Price edits lost on Confirm Group</strong>
<ul><li>Manual room prices now survive <b>Confirm Group</b>. <ul><li>When you set the Unit Price yourself on a Price/Availability line of a new group reservation, the confirmed reservations and the group totals keep your price instead of reverting to the rate code's published price.</li></ul></li><li>Any rate discount on the line still applies on top of the price you enter, so the Unit Price you type is the price before discount. For a rate code in a foreign currency, the amount in the rate code's currency is now filled in as well, so Rate Change shows the right figures.</li><li style="font-weight: bold;">Action required by partners:<ul style="font-weight: normal;"><li>None.</li></ul></li></ul>
<strong>84467 Unable to update Guest Name and Guest List</strong>
<ul><li>Rename a guest on a reservation and the new name now sticks. When you change the guest name and confirm the prompt, the reservation, the guest list, and the guest's member contact all update to the new name — no more re-selecting the guest to force it, and no <b>information on the page is not up-to-date</b> error.</li><li style="font-weight: bold;">Action required by partners:<ul style="font-weight: normal;"><li>None — the fix applies automatically on upgrade.</li></ul></li></ul>
